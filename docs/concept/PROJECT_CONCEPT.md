## Abstract

**The project, Athena Noctua, presents an illustrative demonstration that validates the paradigm of applied anthropomorphism and biomimicry in Machine Learning and Artificial Intelligence architectures. It is a practical representation of the inherent dangers of adversarial manipulation in a latent dimension. Biomimicry in development, design and presentation undermines both the capabilities of AI systems, and the way we receive and evaluate them. The project exposes the potential for dominance over human perception.**

The project is made up of three parts; a video, live demonstration, and provisional algorithm, and is a complementary precursor to *Silent Signal* (forthcoming WIP). The audiovisual piece was accompanied by a live spectrogram analysis and discussion, presented before a live audience. To strengthen the thesis, the work has since evolved into a full exploration framework of time-domain signal hiding using an invertible Neural Network (INN).

### The Narrative

The Narrative of the piece draws on several interconnected references; on first impression the piece references the owl of Minerva, *Athene Noctua*. According to greco/roman mythology the small owl was the sacred companion of Minerva, the goddess of wisdom, allowing her to see truth in the dark. The inaccessible shadow realm also serves as a symbol of retrospective wisdom. Hegel's affirmative metaphor: "The owl of Minerva takes flight only at dusk." Illustrates that any given phase of history can be understood only in retrospect, particularly at the end of an era.

Reflection after the fact allows us to interpret meaning after unfolding events, just as an invertible neural network allows us to articulate hidden structures and explicit initial states. Steganography is the appropriate medium for illustration. From Greek *steganos* meaning covered, and *graphein* meaning writing, I refer to this method of concealing a hidden signal within a host signal.

### Moloch

After the fact, after the network inversion, the underlying 'secret' is revealed; A reference to the symbol of 'Moloch' (מלך), a Canaanite deity historically associated with the practice of child sacrifice, used here as social and political allegory. Over time the figure has since become a philosophical and literary metaphor for any oppressive system, destructive force, or cause that demands extreme human sacrifice (war, oppressive religion, or ruthless political or economic systems). For example; In Fritz Lang's film *Metropolis* (1927 film), the Moloch machine represents the ultimate danger of prioritising technological advancement and profit over human dignity. Moloch has been described as a person operating under cold rationalism,  or as contributinbg to the degeneration of Western civilisation.

This project draws particularly on specific works; John Milton's *Paradise Lost*, where the deity appears prominently as a fallen angel, William Blake's illustration of the poem, Illustration 5 to Milton's "On the Morning of Christ's Nativity": *The Flight of Moloch*. And Allen Ginsberg's unrelenting poem *Howl*, which aptly casts Moloch as the emblem of modern industrialisation; mechanical concrete, metallic, monstrous and soulless. The passage is one of the most famous attacks on modern alienation and dehumanisation in the name of productivity. Consequently manmade systems can become so vast and oppressive that they become void of humanity to the point of becoming sacrificial. Quite aptly reflective of this critical inflection point in human history.

## On Synthetic Silence

 Dusk proves to be a powerful metaphor for the threshold between the tangible and incorporeal. its a transitional moment that creates space for progressive hiding and obscuring of nightfall. Darkness is often characterised by "silence", the sensory gap between a corporeal realm. And so the idea of perception is chalenegd when an auditory void mianifests as an event. An entire dimension exists in the polarity between analogue and synthetic silence. Analogue silence holds a qualitative position in space and time so the register is distinctive in its depth and richness, even in a drak forest. For instance; the hum of electricity and atmospheric fluctuations are residual evidence of tangible subjective experience and human perception in a worldly realm. This assumption reiterates the project narrative, and illustrates further how dangerously misguided it is to attribute anthropomorphic qualities to complex machine systems, that navigate in the dark.

The unique event of analogue silence holds a capacity and shape that bears very little congruence to synthetic silence. Synthetic silence is statistical authored emptiness, whose abstract resemblance is, rather, a mathematical description of absence. Maybe it is true silence in a sense because there is no subject of the experience, no physicality, no externality and no genuine origin. No entropy. Synthetic Silence a distribution proxy of an event.

This is the native condition of the digital dimension, and the project treats it as the material rather than a limitation.

**Sample statistics:**

II. Sign-symmetric distribution about zero (positive and negative sample values in equal measure)
III. Samples represent air-pressure deviation about the ambient level

## Part II

![Film still, roots and hollow, captured in infrared](media/video_screenshots/video_screenshot_10.png)

*Athena Noctua, Presentation Film, captured in infrared, 90 seconds, An audiovisual Live presentation, Reference Point, London 16/03/2026.*

The second part of the project explores the capacity of truly 'hiding' the secret message within a generated synthetic noise floor. A method for signal hiding using a normalising flow-based model, intended to make the same payload genuinely concealed beyond the Human Auditory System (HAS) *and* spectrogram analysis within a generated audio statistically indistinguishable from an independent draw of the same declared distribution.

The proposed architecture is an integer-reversible generative steganographic system that frames payload hiding as constrained sample generation rather than traditional signal modification. At its core is an integer-reversible normalising flow trained by maximum likelihood to learn the distribution of a declared near-silent noise floor, yielding an exact bijection between the noise-floor domain and a simple latent space. Recovery applies the same flow in reverse to reconstruct the latent variables exactly, decode the embedded bitstream, and recover the original payload bit-for-bit, with integrity verified by checksum and spectrogram analysis. Since the mapping is integer-reversible and distribution preserving, every encoded output remains statistically indistinguishable from an ordinary sample of the learned silence distribution while permitting deterministic, lossless recovery without auxiliary side information; fresh encoder randomness ensures that repeated embeddings of the same payload always produce distinct valid silent carriers.

## Algorithm Architecture

![Algorithm Flow Chart](media/diagrams/algorithm_flow_chart.png)

| | Step | |
|---|---|---|
| **I** | SETUP | Define silence spec, host examples $H$ |
| **II** | TRAIN | Fit $\theta$ by maximum likelihood on $H$ |
| **III** | EMBEDDING | $S \rightarrow z \rightarrow$ stego $\tilde{H}$. Generative pass $f_\theta^{-1}$ |
| **IV** | RECOVERY | Same $\theta$, reverse pass: $\tilde{H} \rightarrow \hat{z} \rightarrow \hat{S}$ |
| **V** | VERIFICATION | Checksum $\Gamma$ confirms bit exact match |

*Algorithm Flow Chart.*

### Sequential Steps

**I. Setup**

Define the declared silence spec and collect host examples $H$ from it. This fixes the domain the flow will learn to model, so everything downstream depends on this being an honest, near-silent noise floor.

**II. Training**

Fit the flow parameters $\theta$ by maximum likelihood so that $z = f_\theta(H)$ matches a simple base distribution, with a reconstruction term to keep the integer transform exact.

```
For each batch of H:
    z = f_θ(H)
    ℒ_NLL ← negative log likelihood of z under base distribution
    ℒ_rec ← reconstruction error of integer-reversible pass
    update θ to minimise ℒ = ℒ_NLL + ℒ_rec
```

**III. Embedding**

Turn the payload into a uniform bitstream, pad it out with fresh randomness and redundant latent features to fill the latent space, then run the flow's generative direction to produce the stego signal directly. Nothing here starts from or edits a real host.

```
Input: payload S, trained flow f_θ

    b  = W(C(Γ(S)))        # checksum, compress, whiten
    z  = M(b, r, n)        # r fresh randomness, n padding
    H̃  = f_θ⁻¹(z)          # generative pass

Output: stego signal H̃
```

**IV. Recovery**

Run the same flow forward with the same weights to recover the latent vector exactly, then reverse the embedding steps to get the payload back, with the checksum confirming bit-exact recovery.

```
Input: stego signal H̃, trained flow f_θ

    ẑ  = f_θ(H̃)
    b̂  = payload portion of ẑ
    verify checksum
    Ŝ  = Γ⁻¹(C⁻¹(W⁻¹(b̂)))

Output: recovered payload Ŝ
```

Recovery works because $f_\theta$ and $f_\theta^{-1}$ are exact inverses under the same fixed weights, so no key or side channel is needed. Because $z$ is built to match the flow's base distribution exactly, and $f_\theta$ is trained to map the base distribution to the true distribution of $H$, the output $\tilde{H}$ is a genuine sample of the host distribution.

## Process Schematic

![Process Schematic: image to silence, reversible learned flow](media/diagrams/process_schematic.jpg)

**IMAGE TO SILENCE.** Image payload $\{S\}$ ← canonical raster / vector claim → SHA-256 checksum → lossless compression → written to statistically uniform → map latent base distribution latent, 'measure preserving' CDF → plus fresh randomness.

**REVERSIBLE LEARNED FLOW.** Integer-reversible flow (INN); same weights both directions, $f_\theta$ and $f_\theta^{-1}$ → STEGO.WAV, 24-bit PCM, near silent (kHz against time).

**TRAIN.** Synthetic silence sample "host" $\{H\}$ → sample distribution (training set) → train/fit normalising flow with Maximum Likelihood → $\theta$: the weights = the learned parameters.
